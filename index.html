About

A bit about what I do and how I work.

I’m Inderpreet Singh Bhangu, a software engineer focused on iOS development and low-level Android system engineering. I graduated with a Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science & Engineering from Punjab Technical University in 2024, and I’ve been building production-ready software and open-source systems since my early college years.

Currently, I work as a Frontend Developer at CoreAI Technologies, where I design and build feature-rich applications using SwiftUI, UIKit, Kotlin, and Compose. My work includes Grata Technologies White-Label Projects like Arlington, Chard, Frontrow, and Ironclad. I also work on React Frontend Projects.

Previously, I worked as an iOS Developer at StackGeeks, building apps with SwiftUI, Objective-C, and Firebase—AR live filters (VisionKit), real-time chat, sketching tools, and internal platforms. I care about clean architecture, smooth UX, and scalable code.

Alongside apps, I’ve been contributing to AOSP / custom ROM development since 2017—kernel/device trees/HALs/Qualcomm platforms, bring-up and debugging across kernel versions from 3.10 to 5.15.
